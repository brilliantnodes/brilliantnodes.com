Questions and answers

Short answers about who we are, the networks we work with and what we have built.

What is Brilliant Nodes?

Brilliant Nodes runs nodes and validators for blockchain networks and builds apps on GenLayer. It is run by a team with more than 20 years of experience in financial markets and technology.

Which networks do you run nodes or validators on?

Today we run a Gnosis full node on the main network, a gno.land node on the Onyx test network, and an Ethereum node on the Hoodi test network. We have joined the validator programmes of GenLayer (14 Nov 2026), gno.land (19 Nov 2026) and Flop Network (21 Nov 2026). More on our validator work.

Where are your servers?

Our own servers are in Chicago, São Paulo, Frankfurt, Prague, Johannesburg, Singapore and Melbourne: six continents, with several hosting providers.

Can you support a network on launch day?

Yes. We prepare in fixed steps: a full rehearsal the day before, seed and hub nodes up in three regions three hours before the start, and rented machines across several providers ready 30 minutes before. We have run more than 100 machines at once, in more than 20 countries.

How do you keep validator keys safe?

Owner and operator keys are kept apart: the operator key lives on the validator’s server and the owner key never does. Recovery phrases are kept offline, and every program we run is a pinned version checked against its published hash. More on security.

What have you built on GenLayer?

Launch Claim Checker, ClaimEscrow, PrivateCreditGate and RigSettle, plus three building blocks: ReportReader, RuleCheck and MeterReader. Four have been accepted in GenLayer’s builder programme so far. All the code is public on GitHub. See every project.

Do your demo apps use real money?

No. Every demo uses fictional companies and test tokens only.

How can a network or project contact you?

Email bn@brilliantnodes.com or use the inquiry form on our home page. Security problems can be reported the same way. Open the inquiry form.
